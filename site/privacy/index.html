P Privacy

Your data stays on your line.

Effective 27 August 2026

What the package does

The Python package and CLI read files you select on your own machine. They do not send file contents, schema, keys, usage information, or diagnostics to us. There is no telemetry.

What the browser demo does

The CSV demo processes selected files in browser memory. It does not upload them, store them in local storage, or transmit their contents. Refreshing or closing the tab clears the in-memory data.

Site delivery

Our hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, requested path, time, and user agent to serve and secure the site. We do not use advertising cookies, fingerprinting, or third-party analytics.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project’s public issue tracker. Do not include private dataset contents.
